EXPERIMENT 10: CHAIN-OF-THOUGHT PROMPTING
Model: gemini-flash-lite-latest
Temperature: 0

=== DIRECT PROMPT ===
Prompt: A farmer has 15 cows. He buys 7 more cows. Then he sells half of his cows. How many cows does he have left?
Response: The farmer has **11** cows left. 

Here is the math:
1. He starts with 15 cows.
2. He buys 7 more: $15 + 7 = 22$ cows.
3. He sells half of them: $22 \div 2 = 11$ cows.
Extracted answer: 11

=== CHAIN-OF-THOUGHT PROMPT ===
Prompt: A farmer has 15 cows. He buys 7 more cows. Then he sells half of his cows. How many cows does he have left?
Let's think step by step. Show each step.
Response: Let's break it down step by step:

1. **Start with the initial number of cows:** 
   The farmer starts with 15 cows.

2. **Add the cows he buys:** 
   He buys 7 more cows. 
   $15 + 7 = 22$ cows.

3. **Calculate the cows he sells:** 
   He sells half of his cows. 
   $22 \div 2 = 11$ cows.

4. **Determine how many are left:** 
   He had 22 cows and sold 11, so he has:
   $22 - 11 = 11$ cows left.

The farmer has **11** cows left.
Extracted answer: 11
